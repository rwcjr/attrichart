# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Harassment and personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer privately at robert@blackrifle.company. Reports are handled confidentially.
