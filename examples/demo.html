AttriChart demos

Three configurations of the same engine. Build the library first with pnpm build, then open this file directly in a browser.

1. Marketing attribution, reach mode (default)

The campaign column shows reach, so its segments sum past 100%: a user touched by two campaigns is drawn from both. Where the translucent ribbons stack they darken, which is the shared attribution made visible. From the traffic column rightward the flow consolidates to true unique counts. Hover any ribbon to isolate one path.

2. Same data, fractional mode

With attributionMode: "fractional" a shared cohort splits its width evenly across its campaigns. Every column is strictly additive and sums to 100%, at the cost of the overlap darkening. Use this when arithmetic tidiness matters more than visual emphasis on multi-touch.

3. Four stages, overlap in the middle

The engine is not hardcoded to marketing or to three stages. Here a streaming service journey runs device, genres watched, plan, renewal. The genres stage is the overlap stage: subscribers watch several genres, so that column shows reach while its neighbors stay clean partitions.
